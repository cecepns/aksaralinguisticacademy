Linguistic Programs

We offer a variety of language learning programs designed to meet the needs of learners of different ages, backgrounds, and proficiency levels. Our programs focus on building language skills, enhancing communication, and fostering a deeper understanding of different languages and cultures.

Specialised Program

1. Bahasa Indonesia Program

Indonesian Language for Foreign Nationals

Our Bahasa Indonesia program is designed for foreign nationals who wish to learn Indonesian for daily communication, professional purposes, academic needs, or cultural integration. We offer flexible learning options, including private lessons, group classes, and corporate training, tailored to each learner’s goals and needs.

  • Daily Conversation & Survival Indonesian (shopping, transport, dining, and socialising).
  • Business Indonesian for professional workplace communication, meetings, and emails.
  • Cultural nuances, local customs, and natural communicative expressions in Indonesia.
  • Flexible options: Private 1-on-1 (in-person or online), expat small groups, and corporate sessions.

Early Childhood & Primary

2. Mandarin Program

Mandarin for Early Childhood and Primary School Students

Our Mandarin program is designed for children from nursery through primary school. Through engaging, interactive, and age-appropriate learning activities, students are introduced to Mandarin vocabulary, pronunciation, listening, and speaking skills while developing a strong foundation in the language.

  • Pinyin phonetics & the 4 tones to instill accurate pronunciation habits from early childhood.
  • Multisensory activities: thematic flashcards, children's songs, illustrated stories, and interactive games.
  • Basic Chinese characters (Hanzi) recognition and fundamental stroke order mastery.
  • Supports school curriculum for international, SPK, and national plus school students.

All Ages Welcome

3. Arabic Enrichment Program

Arabic Language Enrichment for All Ages

Our Arabic enrichment program is open to learners of all ages, from children to adults. The program aims to strengthen Arabic language proficiency through structured and engaging lessons tailored to learners’ needs and abilities. Students can develop their Arabic vocabulary, reading, writing, listening, and speaking skills while deepening their understanding of the language.

  • Modern vocabulary acquisition & applied conversational Arabic (Muhadathah).
  • Accurate phonetics (Makharijul Huruf), reading comprehension, and handwriting.
  • Structured grammar foundations (Nahwu & Sharaf) presented simply and practically.
  • Suitable for complete beginners as well as learners deepening classical and modern Arabic literature.

Step-by-Step Mastery

4. English Course

Develop Your English, Step by Step

Our English Course helps learners improve their English for everyday communication, academic success, and professional development. The programme integrates four main language skills: Listening, Speaking, Reading, and Writing. Classes are organised according to learners' current abilities, so students can start at a suitable level and progress gradually.

Includes specialized options for Adult English (personal conversational goals) and Business English (corporate communication, emails, meetings, and presentations).

Structured Learning Pathway

A Learning Pathway for Every Learner

The English Course can be organised into the following levels based on each learner's diagnostic placement:

Starter Level 1

Alphabet sounds, greetings, and basic everyday words.

Beginner Level 2

Simple sentences and everyday practical expressions.

Elementary Level 3

Daily conversations and essential fundamental grammar.

Pre-Intermediate Level 4

Longer conversations and organised descriptive paragraphs.

Intermediate Level 5

Expressing opinions, nuances, and communicating independently.

Upper-Intermediate Level 6

More fluent communication and complex grammatical language.

Advanced Level 7

Academic and professional high-level English command.

Proficiency Level 8

Refined, flexible, and accurate communication at native level.

Adult English Adults

Everyday conversation confidence and tailored personal learning goals.

Business English Corporate

Workplace communication, emails, meetings, and formal presentations.
