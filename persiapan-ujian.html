Test Preparation & Corporate Training

Target-focused intensive training for international English proficiency certifications (IELTS, TOEFL, TOEIC) alongside tailored language training solutions for corporate teams.

International Certification

TOEFL, IELTS & TOEIC Intensive Classes

Prepare with a Clear Strategy

We provide focused preparation for learners who want to improve their English proficiency test performance. Whether for global university admissions, scholarship applications, immigration, or workplace advancement.

IELTS Preparation (Academic & General)

Focused training for Listening, Reading, Writing, and Speaking, with practice and personalized feedback based on the learner's target band score (Band 6.5, 7.0, 7.5+).

TOEFL Practice (iBT / ITP)

Practice in the skills and question types required by the specific TOEFL format supported by the course, with speed-reading strategies and time management techniques.

TOEIC Preparation

Focused on workplace English, particularly business listening, reading comprehension, specialized commercial vocabulary, and test strategies.

Our Preparation Options Include:
  • Individual 1-on-1 private intensive classes
  • Interactive small-group cohorts
  • Diagnostic assessment and customized study planning
  • Flexible course duration tailored to your target exam date

Corporate Language Training

Empowering Teams Through Better Communication

Aksara Linguistic Academy provides language training for companies and professional teams. Our corporate programmes can be tailored to the specific industry, schedule, and objectives of each organisation.

Possible Training Topics Include:
Business English

Professional grammar and corporate communication etiquette.

Professional Email Writing

Polite, effective, and structured correspondence with global partners.

Meetings & Presentations

Leading negotiations, discussions, and confident pitching techniques.

Workplace Speaking & Listening

Fluent cross-cultural communication with international clients.

Training can be arranged on-site at your company's premises or via interactive virtual sessions. Periodic progress reports are delivered to HR / Management.
