Academic School Tuition

Structured academic support for school students across all stages: from early literacy & numeracy (Nursery to K2), to primary school success and secondary analytical mastery.

Early Foundations

1. Nursery to K2 Programme

Building Strong Foundations from an Early Age

This programme helps young children develop essential skills before entering primary school. Lessons are designed to be fun, structured, and appropriate for each child's developmental stage.

Calistung — Bahasa Indonesia
  • Letter recognition and early reading fluency
  • Syllables, words, and sentence reading comprehension
  • Handwriting posture and early motor writing skills
  • Basic counting, quantity awareness, and numeracy
English Phonics
  • Learning English letter sounds accurately
  • Recognising and blending phonetic sounds together
  • Early English reading and spelling confidence
  • Handwriting formation and essential foundational vocabulary

Primary School

2. Primary Tuition

Supporting Academic Success in Primary School

Our Primary Tuition programme helps students understand school subjects more clearly, improve their academic performance, and prepare for school assessments. The programme covers all school subjects, depending on the student's curriculum and learning needs.

"Our goal is not simply to help students finish their homework, but to help them understand what they learn."
  • Explaining difficult concepts in a simple, relatable way.
  • Supporting homework and daily classroom learning.
  • Practising questions and systematically reviewing mistakes.
  • Preparing for tests, midterms, and semester examinations.
  • Building confidence and independent learning habits.

Secondary & High School

3. Secondary Tuition

Stronger Understanding. Better Academic Performance.

Our Secondary Tuition programme provides subject-specific academic support for students who need to strengthen their understanding and prepare for school examinations. Students are encouraged to develop analytical thinking, problem-solving skills, and greater confidence in their academic abilities.

Available Subjects Include:
Mathematics
English
Science
Economics
Accounting
Biology
Physics
Business Studies

Lessons can focus on difficult topics, examination practice, homework support, and systematic revision. We support National Curriculum, Cambridge (IGCSE, A-Levels), and IB.
